Professional Reflection

What this module has taught me

This section is intentionally left as a set of prompts — a reflection is meant to be in your own words, so fill each one in with your real experience, then delete these instructions.

This whole page needs to come from you. Below are the five questions your brief asks for, spaced out so you can write freely under each one. Aim for 300–500 words total across all five. Send me your rough answers (even messy bullet points) if you'd like help tightening the wording — I won't invent the substance for you.
  • 01 — What have you learned during this module?

    [ Write here — think about specific concepts, not just "I learned Java." What clicked for you, and what was the moment it clicked? ]

  • 02 — Which IT professional skill is currently your strongest?

    [ Write here — pick one, and back it up with a specific example from your projects. ]

  • 03 — Which skill would you like to improve?

    [ Write here — be honest. This is more convincing than pretending everything is already strong. ]

  • 04 — What will you do to develop that skill?

    [ Write here — a concrete plan (a course, a side project, practice with a specific tool) rather than "I will practice more." ]

  • 05 — How could this portfolio support your future studies and career?

    [ Write here — how you plan to keep this site updated, and what you'd want it to show a future employer. ]
