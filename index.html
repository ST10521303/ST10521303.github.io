Introduction

Hi, I'm Sihe Masinga, a software development student with a strong interest in technology, problem-solving, and learning how things work. I'm currently building my skills through my studies, where I've been working with Java, object-oriented programming, databases, and other areas of software development.

What I enjoy most about programming is being given a problem and having to figure out how to solve it. I like breaking a problem down into smaller parts, understanding what needs to happen, and then turning that idea into something that actually works. Working on different projects has also taught me that programming is not always about getting everything right on the first attempt. A big part of developing software is testing, finding mistakes, learning from them, and improving the solution.

I'm particularly interested in software development and the possibilities that technology creates. I'm still exploring which areas of the industry I want to specialise in, but I'm interested in backend development, databases, and building practical applications. My goal is to keep expanding my knowledge, become a stronger and more confident developer, and eventually work on software that is useful in the real world.

I consider myself someone who is always looking to improve. Every project gives me an opportunity to learn something new, whether that is a programming concept, a better way of structuring code, or simply learning how to approach a problem differently. I know I still have a lot to learn, and that is part of what makes software development exciting to me.

This portfolio is a reflection of that journey. It contains some of the projects and work I have completed during my studies and shows how my skills are developing over time. As I continue learning and gaining experience, I plan to keep building on it and use it as a record of how far I have come as a developer.
